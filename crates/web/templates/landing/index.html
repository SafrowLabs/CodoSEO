FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need a developer to set this up?

No. Type your site's address and the first check runs in about a minute. There is no plugin to install and no code to add. You only need a developer when a fix has to be made, and CodoSEO writes that fix out for you to forward.

Is CodoSEO better than Screaming Frog?

For watching a site, yes. Screaming Frog is desktop software: someone installs it, runs it by hand, and keeps the laptop open for scheduled crawls. CodoSEO runs in the cloud with nothing to install, checks every site you manage around the clock, and emails you only what changed. It is also AI-native: it fetches your pages as GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot and the other AI crawlers, scores your AI visibility, and writes every fix as a prompt you can hand to a coding agent. Screaming Frog does none of that. Where Screaming Frog still wins is a deep one-off audit of a very large site with JavaScript rendering, so plenty of teams use both: Screaming Frog for the quarterly audit, CodoSEO for every day in between.

What does "AI visibility" mean?

Whether ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and the other AI products can reach and read your site. CodoSEO visits your pages the way their crawlers do, reads what your robots.txt tells them, and checks your pages make sense without JavaScript. If something shuts them out, you get the exact rule to fix it.

Can I share reports with clients or my manager?

Yes. Every site has a score trend and a ranked list of open problems. Export any list as CSV or JSON, or give a teammate access to the site.

Is CodoSEO free?

The free plan watches one site every week with email alerts. Lite at ${{ lite_price }} a month and Pro at ${{ pro_price }} a month add more sites, daily checks and Slack alerts. The software itself is open source, so a developer can also run it on your own server for nothing.

Will it slow down my site?

No. CodoSEO is polite: it obeys your robots.txt, slows down when your server is busy, and names itself CodoSEObot so your developers can spot it in the logs.
