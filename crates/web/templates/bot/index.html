About the crawler

CodoSEObot

CodoSEObot is the crawler behind CodoSEO audits. Someone asked us to check your site, usually its owner or an SEO working for them. It reads public pages to find SEO problems such as broken links, missing titles and noindex tags. It never submits forms, logs in or changes anything.

How politely it crawls

  • At most 5 requests per second and 2 connections at a time, per site.
  • It follows robots.txt, including Crawl-delay (up to 10 seconds) and Disallow rules for CodoSEObot. A site that blocks all crawlers is left alone.
  • On a 429 or 503 it waits for Retry-After and halves its speed. After 20 failures in a row it stops.
  • It reads pages up to 5 MB, never stores a page's HTML, and a free audit stops after 100 pages.

Allow or block it

To keep CodoSEObot out of part of your site, or all of it, add this to robots.txt:

User-agent: CodoSEObot
Disallow: /private/

Use Disallow: / to block it completely. If your firewall or bot protection stops it, allow the user agent above (and the address, if listed) to let audits through.

Synthetic AI fetch checks

After a monitored crawl, CodoSEO may fetch up to {{ probe_pages }} of the pages it just crawled again, using the documented user agents of AI search and user-triggered bots{% if !probe_bots.is_empty() %} ({{ probe_bots.join(", ") }}){% endif %}, and once more as CodoSEObot to compare. This tells the site's owner whether a firewall or CDN turns those bots away when robots.txt lets them in. So you may see an AI bot's user agent arriving from CodoSEO's address{% if ip.is_some() %} above{% endif %}: it is not the bot itself.

  • Every one of these requests carries From: {{ probe_from }}, which is how to reach us about them.
  • It follows robots.txt for the bot token it uses: a page disallowed for that bot is never fetched with its user agent. Requests go one at a time, at least half a second apart, or further apart when robots.txt sets a Crawl-delay (up to 10 seconds).
  • It never runs for the instant audits anyone can start from the home page, only for sites monitored in an account.

To stop it: the site's owner can switch the check off under AI access in their CodoSEO account, and anyone can block CodoSEObot in robots.txt as above, which stops both the crawls and these checks.
