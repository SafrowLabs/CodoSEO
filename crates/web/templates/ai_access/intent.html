How intent works

  • AllowAn issue opens when robots.txt keeps a bot of that kind out.
  • BlockAn issue opens when a bot of that kind can still crawl.
  • No preferenceNever reported either way.

A per-bot override wins over its purpose. Saving re-checks the latest crawl at once, and the issues that opens or resolves send no alerts.
